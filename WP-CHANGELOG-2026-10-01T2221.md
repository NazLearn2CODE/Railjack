## TL;DR

An automated SEO remediation ran against the WordPress REST API during the
night of Oct 1–2. It touched **only the Media Library and post content** —
no core, plugin, theme, config, or user changes. At ~04:00 Oct 2 the hosting
account was rolled back to a pre-operation snapshot (by nat), which undid
every write from this window **except one post edit made at 09:03, after the
restore**. The site was re-scanned at 09:01 and 21:21 Oct 2 and is healthy.
